Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom loved to play outside, but one day, it started to rain. Tom's mom said, "Tom, we need to go inside." But Tom wanted to play outside, so he went to the kitchen.
In the kitchen, Tom saw a big box. He thought, "I can play with the box inside!" So, Tom jumped into the box and started to play. But he was too tired and the rain got stronger. Tom got tired and cold. He wished he had not played outside.
Then, something unexpected happened. The rain stopped and the sun came out. Tom looked at the box and saw that it was now a big, warm sun! The sun was very nice and warm. Tom was happy and started to play with the sun. He forgot about being tired and had a great day with his mom.
